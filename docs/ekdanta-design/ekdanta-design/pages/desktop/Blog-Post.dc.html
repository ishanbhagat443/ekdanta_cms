AA buyer in Pune gets your brochure on WhatsApp. Before he calls you back, he opens your Instagram. What he finds there decides whether he calls you, or the next supplier on his list.

The brochure gets you on the list. What buyers see next decides whether you stay on it.

Buyers check you out before they call

Most buyers do their homework on their phone before they pick it up to call. They look at your website, your Google listing and your Instagram, and they are asking one simple question: is this a real, serious company I can trust with my order?

A brochure answers that question with claims: modern plant, quality products, timely delivery. The trouble is, every supplier says the same things. A reel answers it with proof.

What a brochure can't show

Some things are hard to believe until you see them. A fifteen-second reel can show what pages of text cannot.

THE BROCHURE SAYSTHE REEL SHOWS
“Modern plant” Your machines running at full speed
“Strict quality checks” The gauge, the test and who signs off
“Skilled team” Your people at work, in their own words
“Timely delivery” Trucks loaded and leaving your gate

Every supplier says “quality”. Only a reel can prove it.

Five reels every factory can shoot this month

You don't need a film crew. A recent phone, good light and a plan are enough. Start with these five, in any order.

01 The machine at full speed One machine, one product, start to finish. No talking needed: let the sound of the machine carry it. 15 sec
02 The quality check The gauge, the test and the person who signs off. This is what buyers worry about most. 20 sec
03 Meet the team A supervisor or operator explaining one thing they are proud of, in their own language. 30 sec
04 Order dispatched Packing, loading and the truck leaving your gate. Proof that you deliver, and at what scale. 15 sec
05 Raw material to product A fast-cut journey from raw material to the finished product. Simple, satisfying and easy to share. 20 sec

Keep it simple

Good factory reels are simple. Before you post, run through this list:

  • Shoot vertical, on a phone. A recent phone is enough.
  • Use daylight, or switch on every light on the floor.
  • Keep each reel under 30 seconds.
  • Add captions. Most people watch with the sound off.
  • Put your phone number or WhatsApp link in the caption.

Start small, then build

Start with one reel a week for a month. Watch which ones bring messages and calls, then make more of those. Doing it every week matters more than making it perfect.

And keep your brochure. It still has a job: it is what buyers save, print and forward to their boss. Let your reels bring buyers in, and let your brochure help close the deal.
