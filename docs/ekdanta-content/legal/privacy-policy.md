This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit ekdanta.in, send us a brief, or contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email.

### 2. What we collect

- **When you send a brief:** Your name and phone number, and if you choose, your company or brand name. Also the services, business type and timing you pick. With it, we record the page you sent it from, the website or ad that brought you there, and your IP address, to stop spam and see which of our ads work.
- **When you message, call or email us:** The details and messages you send us.
- **When you browse this site:** Basic technical data such as browser, device, pages visited and time of visit. Analytics and advertising cookies are used only if you allow them.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as bank, health or identity documents through this website. Please do not send them in your messages.

### 3. Why we use it

- To reply to your enquiry, call you back and prepare a proposal or quote.
- To deliver and manage the work if you become a client.
- To keep records the law requires, such as invoices and tax records.
- To see which pages are useful and improve this site, only if you allow analytics cookies.
- To measure our ads, only if you allow advertising cookies.

> **Highlighted note:** We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

### 4. Your consent

We use your personal data with your consent, which you give when you tick the consent box on our brief or message us yourself, and where the law allows it, for example to keep tax records.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

This policy is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 in mind. [Have a lawyer review this policy.]

### 5. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run our business, and only what they need:

- WhatsApp and Meta, when you message us on WhatsApp, or if you allow advertising cookies.
- Google, for analytics if you allow it, and for email and maps.
- Our website hosting and email providers.
- Professional advisers, and authorities when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. We choose providers that protect data with reasonable security safeguards.

### 6. Cookies and analytics

Necessary cookies keep this site working. Analytics cookies (for example Google Analytics) and advertising cookies (for example the Meta Pixel) are switched on only after you allow them in the cookie banner.

You can change your choice at any time from "Cookie settings" in the footer, or by clearing cookies for ekdanta.in in your browser.
